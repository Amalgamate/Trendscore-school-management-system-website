# TrendScore School ERP Website Master Specification
### Premium SaaS Website Architecture, Pages, Design System, Content Hierarchy & Agentic IDE Build Brief

# Project Overview

Build a world-class SaaS website for **TrendScore School ERP**, a comprehensive school management platform designed for Kenyan schools and fully aligned with **Competency-Based Education (CBE)**.

The website should immediately communicate:

> **"TrendScore is the most advanced CBE-ready School ERP in Kenya."**

The design must feel comparable to:
- Stripe
- HubSpot
- Notion
- Monday.com
- Linear
- Zendesk

Avoid the appearance of a traditional school website.

The goal is to present TrendScore as a modern technology platform powering the future of education.
